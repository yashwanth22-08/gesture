Our Mission

IBM NEXUS is the premier cybersecurity and technical innovation club, dedicated to empowering students with hands-on experience in offensive & defensive security, ethical hacking, and competitive programming.

From capture-the-flag competitions to industry workshops and bug bounty programs, we build the next generation of security professionals.
